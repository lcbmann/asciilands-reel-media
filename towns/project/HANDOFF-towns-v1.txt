PIPELINE (as the smithing reel)
Every frame is rendered in Python and piped as raw RGB into ffmpeg/libx264. Cuts and captions anchor to
the cached word times. Silences over 0.25 s at -35 dB are trimmed to 0.30 s after a sentence and
0.20 s after a comma. Two pauses are kept longer: after "religions." (0.55 s, to read the post) and
after "coming along." (0.70 s, the recorded gap was 0.76 s).

SOUND
- Theme from its first note at one level. At "This totally caught me off guard" it switches on a beat
  (24.86 s, 0.08 s before the picture cut) to the game's E major version: a whole step up
  and the same measured loudness (-18.1 LUFS). No fades, except the 1 s fade with the picture at the end.
- Game sound is calibrated per shot (11 LU under the voice) and ducks 6 dB under every word. Short or near
  silent windows are calibrated on RMS, lifted by no more than 20 dB.
- Measured: master -14.1 LUFS, -1.5 dBTP (fixed gain plus limiter). Voice -30.2 LUFS before gain. Game
  14 to 26 dB under the voice while words are spoken. Fort Redleaf, Castle Northmost and the Aphelion window
  have no recorded sound to use (-99 to -105 dB RMS). The raft and world zoom recordings have no sound.
