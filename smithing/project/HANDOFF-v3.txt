PIPELINE CHANGE (as you corrected)
Every frame is now rendered in Python. Gameplay frames or stills are cropped and animated, then captions, the
end card and every other layer are drawn with Pillow alpha_composite. The raw RGB frames are piped into ffmpeg
(-f rawvideo -pix_fmt rgb24 -i -) and encoded with libx264. No ffmpeg overlay or enable filters are used.
