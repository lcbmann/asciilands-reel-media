   Used for: "cross a massive map" (output 1.50 to 2.83 s, shot 01).
   Now: the opening York walk simply continues. That's honest, but it doesn't show scale.
   Film:
   - Use a character that has visited or seen many regions. The World atlas only draws visited or seen regions,

   - Settings: reduced motion OFF, so Local to World animates instead of crossfading.
   - Press M on the Local map and hold about 2 s.
   - Scroll out steadily over 3 to 5 s. Two wheel steps past the widest Local view switches to World.
   - Hold the widest useful World view about 3 s, then move the pointer off the map.

     src='mapzoomout', t=<first moving frame>, speed='fit:<frame where the widest view settles>'

2. oneclickarrows.mkv
   Used for: "But if anyone with the same ingredients can click a button and make exactly the same sword,
   there's not much reason to care who made it." (output 22.90 to 29.17 s, shot 09)
   Now: the Pack's raw materials (Stone Chunk, Copper). Honest, but it doesn't show a click.
   Why arrows, not a stone tool: in the current code, Stone Axe, Pickaxe and Knife are multi-step workbench crafts
   ('workbench_steps', one click per step). Arrows are a true one-click workbench recipe ('simple' mode). Their
   recipe panel says "Crafts immediately from inventory".
   Film at a Workbench:
   - Select Arrows (2 sticks + 1 stone + 1 fibre makes 6 arrows) and hold about 2 s so the ingredients are readable.
   - Click Craft once, then hold 2 to 3 s on the result.
   - Click Craft once more to show it's repeatable, then hold 1 to 2 s.
   - Game sound on, no voice.
   To drop it in: change the "But if anyone" SHOTS entry to src='oneclickarrows', t=<in>, a look that
   shows the recipe card and the Craft button large, and sound=('live', 6.0).

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- I couldn't listen. Sound decisions are based on spectrograms, onset detection, loudness meters and
  level comparisons, not on hearing it. Please listen to the mix, especially the live forge level (+7 dB) under
  the voice, and the music level (-27 dB, -33 dB under live forge sound).
- The motion review used dense frame sequences and contact sheets, not real-time playback.
- 77 s is longer than the 25 to 55 s guideline. The recorded script needs it, and the brief said not to force it short.
- The Cleaver Inspect shots and the final Swordy Maker shot hold a static UI slightly slower than real time
  (0.57x to 1.18x). Nothing moves in those windows, so there's no visible motion change.
- The font is the bundled Atkinson Hyperlegible Bold (Segoe UI isn't available off Windows).
